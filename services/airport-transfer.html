The Details

Essential Information.

We believe in absolute transparency. Here is exactly what to expect when you book with Limore.

What is included?
Chauffeur service, meet & greet, 30 minutes of free waiting time (tracked to your arrival), luggage assistance, complimentary bottled water, and Wi-Fi.
What if my flight is delayed?
We track your flight in real time and adjust our pickup time automatically. No hidden fees for standard delays.
How do I find my driver?
You will receive a full driver profile (photo, car model, license plate) 1 hour before your scheduled pickup. They will meet you at the designated arrivals area.
How do I book for a return trip?
Book online easily, or simply ask your driver to schedule your return trip during your current ride.
